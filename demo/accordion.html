Why this evaluator comes first

The future optimizer and neural network will propose designs like these. These calculations provide a reference for judging the proposals. This experiment evaluates your inputs; it does not yet search for a design or train a model.

What “passes” means here

Panels have zero thickness. Contact in a fully closed flat stack is allowed, but layer order is not validated. Other non-hinge contacts are blocked conservatively. Final box fit is separate from motion: we do not check whether the fold can happen inside that box.
