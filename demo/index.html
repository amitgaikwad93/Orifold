What this experiment models

Two rigid panels, one ideal hinge, and a sheet with zero thickness. Panel lengths and material area stay constant as the hinge rotates. At 180°, the panels are allowed to lie on top of each other.

Endpoint versus motion

The box describes the current configuration. A panel sweeps through space while folding; fitting the final box does not mean the entire motion fits inside it. This experiment does not model thickness, forces, or collision with an enclosure.

Forward geometry first. Inverse design next. Learning after we can measure success.
