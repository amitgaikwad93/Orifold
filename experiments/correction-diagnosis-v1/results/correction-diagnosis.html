Correction Diagnosis

Why fast methods reach at most 46% quality when the answer key reaches 100%. Built from saved validation-v1 records only: no new runs, test set untouched. Quality means valid, every crease on a box wall, and opening at least 90% of the answer key's.

What we found

  1. Correction never tries to improve quality. It only makes designs fit. If the starting design already fits, it is passed through unchanged. That leaves about 130 model answers (17%) and 154 retrieval answers (20%) valid but below quality, never improved.
  2. Correction often closes folds to make a design fit. This is the same shortcut as training run 2. For model starts it accounts for about 100 of the low-opening answers. For nearest-answer starts it accounts for 118 of 145, because retrieval usually starts with a good opening.
  3. The model's own starts are often too closed. In about 105–125 low-opening answers the model began below 90% of the answer key's opening, and correction did not fix it.
  4. Correction ignores walls. About 110 model answers and 55 retrieval answers end valid but with creases floating off the walls.
  5. The remaining invalid answers (8–12%) are stuck searches, not out of budget. Almost all stopped before the 400-evaluation cap, poking out by a median 1.0–1.5% of the sheet. The step-by-step search stalls near the edge.
  6. Two-fold answers are the weak spot. They are the largest group (303 answers) and reach only 12–14% quality from model starts (24% from retrieval). Four-fold answers reach 0–14%.
  7. Model and retrieval starts succeed on different boxes. Each wins about 80–160 answers the other misses. Choosing the better corrected result per box (without using the answer key) could reach about 57% quality. This is a hypothesis to test.
