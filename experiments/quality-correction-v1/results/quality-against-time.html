Quality Against Time

A new correction step that keeps working after a design fits: it opens the folds wide, then brings every crease onto a box wall. Quality means valid, every crease on a wall, and opening at least 90% of the answer key's. The test boxes were not used.

What it shows

  • Quality roughly doubles. Correcting both the model's start and the nearest training answer reaches 92% quality at about 210 ms per answer. The old correction reached 37–46%.
  • Validity is essentially solved for this pipeline: 100% from 400 search steps on.
  • The starting design matters a great deal. A simple start with no model or data stops at 39% quality even with the longest search.
  • The model start does not beat copying the nearest answer. Nearest is ahead at almost every effort level. The two together are better than either.
  • Model alone is unchanged: 39% valid, 22% quality.

Quality reached for the time spent

Each dot is one search limit (25 to 3,200 steps); time is the mean per answer on this machine with four runs in parallel. Hollow marks are earlier methods from the validation benchmark, measured in a separate session, so compare times loosely. The time axis is logarithmic.
